construction The way it all started expand_more

As a child, I was supposed to play games. However, games started to be boring, so I decided to mess with my OS a bit. Enjoyed it.

By the time, Windows was getting more & more annoying. Finally, at the age of 13 I got rid of it. I wanted to full have control over my computer and decide how I maintain my OS.

The more I knew, the more stuff I wanted to control on my own. I entered FOSS world, then started replacing all my proprietary software with open source alternatives. They were actually better.

My whole time at a computer was solving issues, modding my OS, reading source code and finally... creating my own projects! By using open-source software, I feel I'm obliged to give something back to the community.

Later I started building my own PC setups, because my mini PC with iGPU was too weak for 3D Modelling (see this website's background). Of course, enjoyed it.
